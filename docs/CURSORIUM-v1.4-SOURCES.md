## Individual approvals

### Basic

A complete monochrome set with a pencil and clock animation. Original RealWorld templates, assembled by the author.

Original creator declaration; full 15 roles. Two-tone outlines remain readable on both audit backgrounds. Preserve author hotspots and clock timing; no redraw.

Original ZIP: [basic-3.zip](https://www.rw-designer.com/cursor-downloadset/basic-3.zip); published 2019-11-21. SHA256 `ad954f84bc20d38b52dc2ac26f6e038f05cadfa069bc8211cc3edd62520bf455`.

### Dot Minimal

A compact geometric dot with distinct resize, text and busy roles.

Original dot design; 15 explicit source roles. White/black outlines distinguish small shapes on both audit backgrounds. Busy/Working 3-jiffy rotation retained; not a flashing full-screen effect.

Original ZIP: [dot-minimal.zip](https://www.rw-designer.com/cursor-downloadset/dot-minimal.zip); published 2021-02-12. SHA256 `7ea7fb6d365e0b77d6eddc2f7d582e8f252c4359cab0bdf025e064883d43fc15`.

### GOLDEN

Golden arrows, metallic accents and animated busy indicators. Includes original optional variants.

Original generic geometry; explicit diagonal orientation visually reviewed (LR=NWSE, RL=NESW). Gold Text is thin; stronger contrast on dark backgrounds. Full 15 roles, no inherited artwork; author fixed Busy hotspot in source update.

Original ZIP: [golden-2.zip](https://www.rw-designer.com/cursor-downloadset/golden-2.zip); published 2023-07-21. SHA256 `1a8f669e468b97dc08f56f47633adc47a1e57e1f80bc5c333583ba25808d1efb`.

### AmberRose

Warm rose pointers with double outlines and original animated clock. Right-handed defaults; original left-handed extras retained.

Creator explicitly permits cursor-manager inclusion; 15 roles with two-tone outlines. Reviewed default right-handed resources, 10-jiffy Busy clock. No artwork or hotspots altered.

Original ZIP: [amber-rose.zip](https://www.rw-designer.com/cursor-downloadset/amber-rose.zip); published 2026-05-07. SHA256 `e6c40e0f044bf8e6e27f651e62f89b94d4d181723aa5fad037872792297ec064`.

### Brown Cow

Hand-drawn little cows with coordinated resize, text and animated selection roles. All original variants retained.

Creator gift for mother, original cow artwork; 15 native roles. Normal uses author top-left marker, Text beam and resize directions reviewed. Contrasting brown outlines and pale fill on both backgrounds. Source timing 6-90 jiffies retained.

Original ZIP: [brown-cow.zip](https://www.rw-designer.com/cursor-downloadset/brown-cow.zip); published 2020-12-16. SHA256 `a9c9204cfba59e49ca9196b3ebd06e0dba7d00072fa0f6778ea7c5c31db5b9f3`.

### Little Chicken

Original Easter chicks, eggs and animated directional poses. Attribution to Sirea; all original bonus files retained.

Author identifies own chicken icon work. CC attribution retained with original source link. Full native role map; beak/egg/resize hotspots reviewed against source first frames. Original ZIP: [little-chicken.zip](https://www.rw-designer.com/cursor-downloadset/little-chicken.zip); published 2011-03-25. SHA256 `9bb7873a7fd151d52581d6d285d39b275289add9d1b84f286987e9d10b35392b`.

### Discrete

Orange and yellow triangular pieces with original animated rotations. Original left-handed variants included.

Original author and explicit 3D design description; CC attribution and source retained. Full 15 roles. Centered resize/crosshair and pointed arrow hotspots verified. Dotted beam is intentionally subtle; orange/yellow endpoints remain visible on both backgrounds.

Original ZIP: [discrete.zip](https://www.rw-designer.com/cursor-downloadset/discrete.zip); published 2009-04-18. SHA256 `8ab72c9e540ff224ec244f61deea56645bd8d2286aa45d3be333c67dbbfea425`.

## Evidence and limitations

acquisition-ledger-1.1.0.json records every candidate, rejection reason, original ZIP/page hash and approved package hash. native-audit-1.1.0.json records every downloaded CUR/ANI hash, native dimensions/hotspot and ANI timing. The review JSON contains all explicit filename role mappings, independent of archive enumeration order. Contact sheets compare original initial frames on white/navy; they do not establish full-duration animation accessibility or long-term ergonomics. Soft pastel and subtle dotted-beam limitations are disclosed above. Full hardware/mixed-DPI or long-term user trials are not claimed.
